Community Guidelines & Safety

Standards of conduct in Shroud's Lockin Crib

1. Focus on Growth & Accountability

This crib exists to help builders, hackers, and students maintain consistency. Respect each other's grind and share genuine progress.

2. Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Abuse

Hate speech, threats, harassment, bullying, and targeted attacks are strictly forbidden. Server-side filters actively flag and block prohibited content.

3. Minor Safety & Appropriate Content

Explicit, adult, or sexually suggestive material is strictly prohibited. While automated filters mitigate harmful text, human moderators review reports.

4. Authentic Logging

Log real hours and authentic accomplishments. Streak manipulation and spamming artificial dates undermines the core spirit of self-mastery.

5. Moderation & Enforcement

Violations will result in warnings, chat muting, content deletion, or permanent account suspension at administrator discretion.
